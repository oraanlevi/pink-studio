How It Works
01 Review

We walk through your existing site together — what's holding it back visually, what's unclear in the content, and what your audience needs to understand immediately.

You leave this phase with a clear picture of what gets changed and why.

02 Refine

Design and development happen in parallel. You review at each milestone and refine until the updated site feels like a true upgrade — not just a paint job.

We don't relaunch until you're proud of it.

03 Relaunch

All updates go live with full cross-device testing. Your existing traffic and SEO are preserved. One week of post-launch support follows to address anything that comes up.

Clean handoff, no loose ends.

A Refresh works with your existing site structure — we update, improve, and elevate rather than rebuild from scratch. It's right for brands whose bones are solid but whose site no longer reflects where they are. If you need a fundamentally different experience, the Signature build is the better fit.

We're careful to preserve your existing URL structure and meta setup during any refresh. Performance improvements (faster load times, better mobile experience) often have a positive effect on SEO rather than a negative one.

Access to your current site files or CMS, your brand assets, and a clear sense of what you want to improve. If you have updated photography or copy ready, great — if not, we'll work with what you have and identify what might be worth adding.
