How It Works
01 Review

Every Refresh begins with a collaborative review of the existing website. Together we go through what's working, what no longer reflects the business, what should be removed, and what should be added.
We also look at why the current site isn't performing as well as it could: whether that's a layout issue, a messaging issue, or something technical.

Nothing changes until we agree on the plan.

02 Refine

Based on the review, we implement the agreed improvements. The scope varies by project. A Refresh might involve typography, layout refinements, mobile optimization, navigation improvements, new sections, image replacement, motion and interactions, or accessibility work.
Only the work your site actually needs.

Nothing extra. Nothing missed.
