Pink Web Studio Privacy Policy Effective September 11, 2026

Pink Web Studio is a web design studio based in Los Angeles, operated by Oraan Levi. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information collected through pinkwebstudio.com. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide. When you submit the contact form or the service quiz on this website, we receive the name, email address, service interest, and message you enter.

We do not create user accounts, require registration, or collect payment information through this website. We do not purchase or obtain personal information from third-party data brokers.

Like all web servers, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records standard server logs when you visit the site, which may include your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and pages visited. These logs are used for security and operational purposes.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the contact form or quiz is used solely to respond to your inquiry and understand which services may be relevant to you.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or profiling. We may disclose information to third-party service providers only as necessary to operate this website, process your inquiry, and provide our services — for example, to transmit your form submission to us. Those providers are listed below.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics services. We do not set cookies to track your behavior across websites.

Google Fonts are loaded on every page of this site directly from Google's servers. When your browser requests a font file, Google receives your IP address and browser information as a standard part of that request. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Formspree — processes form submissions on our behalf. When you submit the contact form or quiz, your data is transmitted to and stored by Formspree. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves typography files on every page. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts this website. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly — linked from this site for booking discovery calls. Calendly is an independent service; if you visit their site, their privacy policy applies. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.

Pink Web Studio does not control the data practices of these services and is not responsible for their privacy policies.

Data Retention

Form submissions are stored by Formspree according to their own retention policies. Pink Web Studio does not maintain a separate database of form submissions beyond what Formspree retains.

If you would like your submission deleted, please contact us at studio@pinkwebstudio.com and we will request deletion from Formspree on your behalf.

Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted through this site. We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you may have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information has been collected and to request deletion.

To exercise any of these rights, email studio@pinkwebstudio.com.

Security

All pages on this website are served over HTTPS. Form submissions are transmitted to Formspree over an encrypted connection. While we take reasonable steps to protect information, no method of internet transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, please contact us and we will arrange for its deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
