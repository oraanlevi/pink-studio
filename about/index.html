In her own words

I didn't build Pink Web Studio because I wanted to make websites.

I built it because I design like I'm creating a place, not a page. I've always been drawn to typography, movement, materials, and the details that quietly shape how a space feels. Every project begins there. The website comes later.

Luxury isn't a look. It's the feeling someone has when every detail has been considered. I believe the best websites do more than look beautiful. They build trust, create emotion, and leave a lasting impression.

Unlike working with a large agency, you'll work directly with me from our first conversation through launch day. Every decision is made collaboratively, and nothing goes live unless it's something I'm proud to put my name on.

If you're reading this, you probably already know whether we're a good fit.

Oraan Levi Founder, Pink Web Studio

I started Pink Web Studio because I felt there was a growing lack of intention online. Too many websites were trying to do more, say more, and show more. The brands that inspired me most did the opposite. They understood that every detail mattered.
