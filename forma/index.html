Why?

I created FORMA because I wanted to reimagine how interior designers manage projects. Instead of relying on Pinterest for inspiration, spreadsheets for tracking, Dropbox for files, and email for communication. FORMA brings the entire workflow together in one thoughtfully designed platform.

The result.

Interior design projects deserve software built specifically for the way they evolve. FORMA brings every stage of the process together in one calm, connected workspace.
