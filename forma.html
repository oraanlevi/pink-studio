Why?

I created FORMA because I wanted to reimagine how interior designers manage projects. Instead of relying on Pinterest for inspiration, spreadsheets for tracking, Dropbox for files, procurement trackers for orders, and email for communication, FORMA brings the entire workflow together in one thoughtfully designed platform.

Success.

Interior design projects deserve software built specifically for the way they evolve. FORMA brings every stage of the process together in one calm, connected workspace.

Mood Boards

A flexible visual workspace where designers pin inspiration, products, and references into spatial boards. Images can be pulled from the web or uploaded directly, then annotated and organized by room or concept.

Selections

Products move from inspiration into organized specifications: vendor, SKU, dimensions, finish, quantity, and price. All structured and ready for client presentation without any reformatting.

Client Portal

Designers share a curated selection deck directly with clients. Clients review, leave comments, and approve or request changes, all in one place, with no email attachments or PDF confusion.

Procurement

Once a selection is approved, it becomes a tracked order. Forma manages the full procurement lifecycle from placed to received, so designers always know where every piece stands without digging through email threads.

Timeline

Important project milestones like approvals, orders, deliveries, and installations automatically build a living historical record. The timeline becomes a project diary that both the designer and client can reference at any point.

Files

Contracts, floor plans, renderings, invoices, and drawings live inside the project, not in a separate Dropbox folder or email attachment. Every document is one click away from the work it belongs to.
