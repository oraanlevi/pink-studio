The Challenge

Design an interior design studio that feels timeless instead of trend-driven. I wanted June House to feel like a studio that creates homes people can live in for decades, not spaces designed around short-lived trends. The challenge was communicating warmth, craftsmanship, and quiet luxury without making the experience feel overly styled.

The Approach

Let the materials tell the story. I designed every page to feel like walking through a beautifully collected home. Natural textures, architectural photography, generous white space, and restrained typography allow every project to breathe. Rather than filling the website with decoration, I wanted each detail to feel intentional and carefully considered.

The Outcome

A website that feels like stepping inside the home before it's even built. The experience encourages visitors to slow down and imagine how they want their own home to feel. Instead of showcasing beautiful interiors alone, the website communicates the studio's philosophy and the experience of working with them.
